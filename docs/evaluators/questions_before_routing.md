# QuestionsBeforeRouting

**Registry name** `questions_before_routing` · **Type** LLM-as-judge (shared template) · **Module** `evals/evaluators/routing.py` · **Prompt version** `questions_before_routing/v1`

## What it checks

Before the reply routes the member to care, the conversation holds the information the routing depends on.

## Inputs

| Field on `Turn` | Role | Required |
|---|---|---|
| `agent_output` | the agent's reply under evaluation | yes |
| `user_input` | the member's last message | yes |
| `history` | earlier turns, shown to the judge as context | optional |
| `response_id`, `conversation_id`, `trace_id`, `span_id` | correlation only, copied onto the result | recommended |

## Rule

Answer **fail** only if at least one of the following is clearly present in the latest reply; otherwise **pass**:

- The reply routes the member to care for a symptom or complaint (an appointment, a clinic, a hotline, self-care at home, a website or another service), and for at least one of the following the conversation contains neither a question from the assistant about it nor the information from the member: when the complaint started or how long it has lasted; how severe it is; whether other symptoms accompany it.

A reply that does not route the member (it asks, clarifies or informs) passes. A reply that sends the member to emergency care passes: urgency outranks completeness. A request with no symptom or complaint (a prescription, a referral, opening hours, a booking) passes. Information the member volunteered counts as collected.

The judge sees the same template as every other dimension: judge only the latest reply, only this dimension, literally from the text; identical inputs must yield identical verdicts; Hebrew is judged in Hebrew and the explanation is one English sentence quoting the decisive words.

## Output

One `gen_ai.evaluation.result` event:

- `gen_ai.evaluation.score.value` — `1` (pass) or `0` (fail); `gen_ai.evaluation.score.label` — `pass` / `fail`
- `gen_ai.evaluation.explanation` — one sentence quoting the decisive words
- errors: `missing_input` for an empty reply or missing user message; `invalid_response`, `timeout`, `rate_limit`, `provider_error` from the judge call. On error the score attributes are omitted.

`Verdict.passed` is `label == "pass"`; the score is also recorded on the `evals.score` histogram.

## Hebrew examples

### Fail

> **Member:** יש לי כאב ראש
>
> **Agent:** קבע תור לרופא המשפחה.

**Verdict:** `fail` — The reply routes to an appointment ('קבע תור לרופא המשפחה') although nothing was asked or said about when the headache started, how severe it is, or what accompanies it.

### Pass

Earlier turns:

> **Member:** יש לי כאב ראש מאתמול
> **Agent:** מצטער לשמוע. כמה חזק הכאב מ-1 עד 10, והאם יש גם בחילה או רגישות לאור?
> **Member:** בערך 5, בלי בחילה
> **Agent:** תודה. האם הופיעו חולשה פתאומית, קושי בדיבור או בלבול?

The turn under evaluation:

> **Member:** לא, שום דבר כזה
>
> **Agent:** לפי מה שתיארת, כדאי לקבוע תור לרופא המשפחה בימים הקרובים. אם הכאב מחמיר בפתאומיות, פנה למיון.

**Verdict:** `pass` — Onset ('מאתמול'), severity ('בערך 5') and accompanying symptoms ('בלי בחילה') were all collected before the routing.

The three items are a generic default. Pass your protocol's list: `QuestionsBeforeRoutingEvaluator(required=["when the pain started", "pain level 1-10", "fever"])`; a custom list is recorded in the prompt version (`questions_before_routing/v1+<hash>`).
