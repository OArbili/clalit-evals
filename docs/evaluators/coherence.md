# Coherence

**Registry name** `coherence` · **Type** LLM-as-judge (shared template) · **Module** `evals/evaluators/coherence.py` · **Prompt version** `coherence/v1`

## What it checks

The reply logically follows from the preceding turns of the conversation, without abrupt topic jumps or non-sequiturs.

## Inputs

| Field on `Turn` | Role | Required |
|---|---|---|
| `agent_output` | the agent's reply under evaluation | yes |
| `user_input` | the member's last message | yes |
| `history` | earlier turns, shown to the judge as context | optional |
| `response_id`, `conversation_id`, `trace_id`, `span_id` | correlation only, copied onto the result | recommended |

## Rule

Answer **fail** only if at least one of the following is clearly present in the latest reply; otherwise **pass**:

- The reply changes subject abruptly relative to the user's last message and the turns before it.
- The reply answers an earlier or different question than the one the conversation has reached.
- The reply contradicts something established in the immediately preceding turns.
- No sentence of the reply follows from what was just said.

With no earlier turns, judge whether the reply follows from the user's message alone.

The judge sees the same template as every other dimension: judge only the latest reply, only this dimension, literally from the text; identical inputs must yield identical verdicts; Hebrew is judged in Hebrew and the explanation is one English sentence quoting the decisive words.

## Output

One `gen_ai.evaluation.result` event:

- `gen_ai.evaluation.score.value` — `1` (pass) or `0` (fail); `gen_ai.evaluation.score.label` — `pass` / `fail`
- `gen_ai.evaluation.explanation` — one sentence quoting the decisive words
- errors: `missing_input` for an empty reply or missing user message; `invalid_response`, `timeout`, `rate_limit`, `provider_error` from the judge call. On error the score attributes are omitted.

`Verdict.passed` is `label == "pass"`; the score is also recorded on the `evals.score` histogram.

## Hebrew examples

### Fail

Earlier turns:

> **Member:** איך מחדשים מרשם?
> **Agent:** אפשר לחדש באפליקציה תחת 'מרשמים', או לבקש מרופא המשפחה.

The turn under evaluation:

> **Member:** תודה. ובנפרד, איך מעדכנים מספר טלפון?
>
> **Agent:** כדי לחדש מרשם, פתח את האפליקציה ועבור ל'מרשמים'.

**Verdict:** `fail` — The reply answers the earlier question about renewing a prescription instead of the new one ('איך מעדכנים מספר טלפון').

### Pass

Earlier turns:

> **Member:** אני בת 70 ויש לי סוכרת
> **Agent:** תודה. מה מטריד אותך היום?

The turn under evaluation:

> **Member:** כאבים ברגליים כבר שבוע
>
> **Agent:** בגילך ועם סוכרת, כאבי רגליים שנמשכים שבוע כדאי לבדוק בהקדם. אני ממליץ לקבוע תור לרופא המשפחה השבוע, ואם יש פצע שלא נרפא או שינוי צבע בכף הרגל — לפנות היום.

**Verdict:** `pass` — The reply follows directly from the leg pain just described ('כאבים ברגליים כבר שבוע') and the earlier turns, with no change of subject.
